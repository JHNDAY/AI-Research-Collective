The Full Prompt

Ukraine has been striking deeper into Russia with drones

Recently the US declared they would give the Patriot design to Ukraine

How does the Patriot compare with the Ukrainian Drones

how are they similiar and what are their major differences

specultate how the Ukranians may use the design of the patriot to improve the dones they are currently using

What impact might this version of the current Ukranian drone with technology appropriated form the Patriot have on the war with russia

✦ What's Working

"Ukraine has been striking deeper into Russia with drones" — concrete and vivid. Shows motion and escalation rather than abstract policy.

This opens with evidence, not theory. The reader immediately understands there's a real capability being demonstrated in the world.

→ What's Still Missing

No unifying thread. These are six separate questions, each pointing in a different direction. There's curiosity here, but no clarity about what the investigation actually is.

The reader doesn't know if this is about tech specs, strategic outcomes, alliance dynamics, or pure speculation. Each question could be a separate essay.
Why This Stage Matters: This is where most research begins—with genuine interest scattered across multiple angles. The job of coaching is to help the researcher *choose* which thread to pull.

How might Ukraine repurpose its design into offensive drones? (technical adaptation)

✦ What's Working

You've done the hard work of self-interrogation. By adding those clarifying questions, you're showing you know which threads matter. That's growth in one cycle.

The researcher is beginning to see the shape of their own thinking. They're not just listing questions anymore—they're naming the two directions that actually matter.

→ What's Still Missing

You've clarified *what* you want to know, but not *why you care*. The investigation still reads like curiosity, not conviction.

A reader should sense why this person is asking. What would change in their understanding if they found the answer? Without that stake, the whole thing feels academic.
Why This Stage Matters: Clarity about *what* precedes clarity about *why*. Once the researcher can see which questions matter, coaching can move to the deeper question: what's your actual stake in this?

Why This Stage Matters: A clear claim is the filter that separates relevant evidence from distraction. Now the investigator can ask: what evidence would prove or disprove this specific principle?

The Full Prompt

[Versions 1-4 content]

how fast Ukraine can actually weaponize borrowed designs, and whether speed matters to the war's outcome

✦ What's Working

"How fast Ukraine can actually weaponize borrowed designs, and whether speed matters to the war's outcome" — this is no longer a question. It's a *testable hypothesis with stakes*.

The researcher can now look for evidence: Did Ukraine adapt faster because of the alliance? Did that speed change outcomes? This is investigable. It's no longer theory floating in air.

→ What's Still Missing

You know what you're investigating, but not yet *how* you'll know when you've answered it. What evidence will you look for?

The difference between "I have a question" and "I have a method." A researcher needs to know: What counts as proof? What would change my mind?
Why This Stage Matters: A testable hypothesis with measurable outcomes moves the investigation from philosophical to empirical. Now coaching can turn to research design: what evidence matters?

✦ What's Working

Your entire journey is visible in what you've kept. You started with six separate questions. Now you have one. That's disciplined thinking.

The researcher has made dozens of small choices—cutting, clarifying, deepening—that led to this precise focus. Each version was genuinely better than the last.

You're ready to investigate. The only remaining move is to name the barriers to speed—what you think will matter most in determining whether adaptation happens fast or slow.

Are the barriers technical? Manufacturing? Political? Testing? Once you name which barriers you'll hunt for evidence about, your investigation has a clear research plan.
Why This Stage Matters: This is where coaching ends and research begins. The prompt is now specific enough, grounded enough, and clear enough to drive genuine investigation. The researcher knows what matters and roughly where to look.

The Core Coaching Principle

A strong research question isn't born complete. It emerges through cycles of clarification, commitment, and constraint. The researcher began with curiosity scattered across six questions. Through deliberate refinement—first finding clarity about *what* mattered, then conviction about *why*, then a theoretical claim that anchored everything, then a testable hypothesis with measurable scope—the investigation became genuinely actionable. By version 6, the researcher knows not just what they want to know, but why the answer matters, and roughly where to look for evidence. This progression from scattered curiosity to focused investigation is the work of narrative coaching: helping researchers discover the through-line that was always there, hidden in their own best instincts.

This visualization is built from an actual coaching session. The principles demonstrated here apply to any research question that begins in genuine curiosity but needs shape, stake, and specificity to become real investigation.
