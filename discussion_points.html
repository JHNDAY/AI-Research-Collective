💬 Discussion Points

AI Hardware Market 2026 — Five Questions to Spark Conversation

1
Data Center Economics & the Environment

We learned that hyperscalers are spending $600-800 billion annually on AI infrastructure by 2026. What are the environmental costs of this buildout — power consumption, water cooling, land use — and who should bear that responsibility?

2
The Perceptron Parallel

In 1958, the Perceptron was called a "thinking machine" by the popular press, but it turned out to be far more limited than promised. How is today's AI hype cycle similar or different? Are we making the same mistakes?

3
Vendor Lock-in & Independence

We saw that hyperscalers like Google and Amazon are building their own custom ASICs instead of relying on NVIDIA. As a consumer, should you worry about companies becoming too dependent on their own proprietary chips? What could go wrong?

4
The Cost of AI at Scale

If it costs $10-12 billion to build an operational data center, and $15-35 billion for planned facilities, who can afford to compete in AI? Does this create a monopoly problem where only the richest companies can participate?

5
Edge vs. Cloud Privacy

Your Pixel 11 Pro runs AI models locally (edge processors) for some tasks, but sends data to Google's cloud for others. How should we decide which AI processing should stay on your device versus going to the cloud? Who should make that choice?
