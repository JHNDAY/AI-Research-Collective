Literary Works

📖The Tell-Tale Heart — Edgar Allan Poe
A descent into madness and guilt as a murderer is haunted by his victim's persistent heartbeat.
📖Arrangement in Black and White — Dorothy Parker
A scathing satire that exposes racial prejudice lurking beneath polite, progressive-minded conversation.
📖The Lottery — Shirley Jackson
A chilling tale of how ordinary communities participate in ritualized violence without questioning tradition.
📖The Old Man and the Sea — Ernest Hemingway
An aging fisherman's epic struggle with nature, endurance, and the meaning of victory in defeat.
📖To Kill a Mockingbird — Harper Lee
A coming-of-age story exploring racism, morality, and justice in the American South through a child's eyes.

History & Politics

Lincoln's Second Inaugural Address — Abraham Lincoln
Lincoln's profound meditation on providence, suffering, and reconciliation as the Civil War nears its end.
House Divided Speech — Abraham Lincoln
Lincoln argues that a nation cannot endure permanently divided and must resolve the slavery question.

Health Care

How a Sprawling Hospital Chain Ignited Its Own Staffing Crisis — The New York Times
Investigation of how aggressive corporate cost-cutting and management decisions caused massive staff shortages.
They Were Entitled to Free Care. Hospitals Hounded Them to Pay — The New York Times
How nonprofit hospitals pursue vulnerable patients legally entitled to free care through aggressive collection tactics.

Science & Philosophy

The Scientist's Conscience: The Moral Un-Neutrality of Science — Charles P. Snow
Snow argues that science is fundamentally moral and scientists bear responsibility for nuclear weapons and global inequality.
Computing Machinery and Intelligence — Alan M. Turing
The foundational paper introducing the Turing Test and arguing that machines can exhibit intelligent behavior.
