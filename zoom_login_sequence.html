💡 Tips for BOLLI Members

  • Do this once: The initial setup takes just one meeting—after that, it's effortless.
  • Bookmark the link: Save your meeting URL to your browser favorites for quick access.
  • Test your audio/video: Join a few minutes early to test your microphone and camera before the discussion begins.
  • You're automatically recognized: Once you're logged into the Zoom app, facilitators Sally, George, and John can track attendance and identify participants.
  • Stay logged in: Don't log out of the Zoom app between sessions—you'll stay logged in automatically for each Wednesday's meeting.
