Sharing a Claude chat

Every chat starts private. Here is how to share one, step by step, using the actual Claude screens. Steps 1 and 2 share with people you invite by email. Steps 3 to 5 turn on a public link that anyone can open.

  1. Click Share

    In the upper right corner of the chat, click Share. The Share session panel opens.

  2. Invite people by email

    The panel says that only messages up until now will be shared. Anything you write later stays private.

    • Access starts as Only people invited.
    • Type an address in Add people by email and click Invite.
    • Copy link gives you a link that opens only for the people you invited.
  3. Want a link anyone can open? Continue here.

  4. Change who has access

    Click the Only people invited row, or the gear icon at the bottom. This opens Share chat settings.

    Open the Who has access menu and choose Anyone with the link.

  5. Read the cautions, then Save

    Claude shows three reminders before you make the link public:

    • No sign-in needed. Anyone with the link can open it, and you won't see who did.
    • Links travel. People can forward or copy it. Turning the link off later can't take back copies.
    • Not listed, not invisible. Search engines are asked not to index it, but reposts are out of Claude's control.

    Click Save.

  6. Copy the public link

    Back in the Share session panel, access now reads Anyone with the link, and the button changes to Copy public link.

    Click it and paste the link into an email, a message, or a course page.

    Only share chats you'd be comfortable posting publicly. Leave out personal details and other people's content.

What viewers see

  • The chat as it was when you shared it.
  • Artifacts are included. Attached files are not.
  • Viewers can read the chat but can't continue it.

To stop sharing a public link

Open Share again, go to the access settings, switch back to Only people invited, and click Save. The public link stops working.
